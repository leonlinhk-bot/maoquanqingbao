Regulator cracks down on 'quick returns' life products

By AIR team | 16 Oct 2026

China Life & Health Regulation

China's insurance regulator has issued a "Notice on Carrying Out Investigation and Rectification of Quick-Returns Products" to all life insurance companies, launching a special probe into such products. The crackdown is undertaken at a time when life insurers are commencing or preparing to start sales for 2027.
