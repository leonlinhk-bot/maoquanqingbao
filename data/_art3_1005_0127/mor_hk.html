Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Media OutReach Newswire, press release distribution, AI visibility, and more.

Why should I choose Media OutReach Newswire? 

Media OutReach Newswire delivers your press releases directly to the inboxes of verified journalists in our curated global media database, increasing your earned media opportunities. Our guaranteed news postings on real media sites and Schema Markup improve your SEO and GEO for AI visibility. Post-release reports provide you with actionable data insights, while our pioneering PR Campaign Intelligence allows you to showcase your strategic communications impact to C-suite decision makers.

Which countries does Media OutReach Newswire distribute to?

Media OutReach Newswire provides global press release distribution to over 190 countries and territories, covering USA & Canada, Latin America, UK & Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia Pacific, Southeast Asia and ASEAN. With us, on top of guaranteed online news postings, your press releases reach the inboxes of 200,000+ journalists and editors at 70,000+ media titles in over 40 languages.

Does Media OutReach Newswire offer guaranteed media coverage?

Yes. Media OutReach Newswire is the only global newswire that can guarantee that your press releases are published verbatim on real news media sites with trusted domains. This provides you with full control of key messages and gives your brand greater AI visibility compared to syndication and low-authority sites commonly used by other wires.

Do press releases improve AI search visibility for brands?

Yes. At Media OutReach Newswire, we integrate JSON-LD Schema Markup, which is structured data that helps AI answer engines understand your press releases and see them as trusted sources of brand information. Furthermore, by distributing through us, your press releases are published on real news media sites with domain authority. This combination of structured data and news sites with domain authority enables AI to find, trust, and cite your brand – improving your AI search visibility.

How do I ensure my press releases appear in AI search?

To appear in AI search, use clear, factual language, authoritative information, the inverted pyramid and “who, what, where, when, why and how” principles of a press release, and structured formats. At Media OutReach Newswire, we integrate JSON-LD Schema Markup into all press releases that we distribute. This helps AI engines understand your press release content, making them appear in AI search results. To further enhance AI visibility, releases should be distributed frequently and be published on real news sites with domain authority.

How quickly can I distribute a press release with Media OutReach Newswire?

Distribution can be arranged promptly once your content, distribution geographies and targeted trade categories are confirmed. As a new client, when the contract is signed and payment settled, you are provided with login credentials to our Media OutReach Newswire online portal. Usually, this process takes only 1-2 hours.

How much does press release distribution cost?

It depends on content length, distribution scope, countries, languages and multimedia. The most cost-effective option is to use a global newswire like Media OutReach Newswire with our transparent pricing structure. With us, you enjoy a single fee for general news and unlimited trade news beats with absolutely no hidden charges for additional trade categories. We also offer packages with discounts on our rate card. Plus, our post-release distribution reports with journalist and data insights and our pioneering PR Campaign Intelligence are built-in, which saves you time on tracking and reporting your results.

What is a newswire and how does it help press release distribution?

A newswire distributes news and announcements from organisations – in the form of press releases – to journalists, editors and newsrooms. Key advantages of using a newswire include instant and wide distribution of press releases to large numbers of news sites with domain authority. This builds brand awareness and trust with your target audiences. Media OutReach Newswire delivers your release directly to the inboxes of verified journalists in our database, who view us as a trusted news source. This increases journalist engagement and earned coverage; it builds brand reputation and can make your news go global.

What are the key differences between Media OutReach Newswire and other newswires?

Media OutReach Newswire delivers your press release directly to the inboxes of verified journalists in our proprietary, curated database. It is also the only global newswire that can guarantee that your press releases are published on real media sites with domain authority. This is a powerful advantage for SEO, GEO and AI visibility. The ready-to-use post-release reports and pioneering PR Campaign Intelligence save PR teams time, provide you with actionable data and journalist insights, and empower you to report boardroom-ready results with confidence.
