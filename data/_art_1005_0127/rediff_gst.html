Employers May Claim ITC on Employee Insurance GST

By Rediff Money Desk, New Delhi
1 Minute Read Listen to Article
Share:    

Oct 04, 2026 19:28

x
The GST Council is set to consider allowing employers to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on GST paid for employee insurance premiums, potentially reducing costs.

Key Points

  • The GST Council will consider allowing employers to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on employee insurance premiums.
  • Currently, businesses pay 18% GST on group insurance policies for employees but cannot claim ITC.
  • This proposed change aims to make employee insurance cheaper for employers.
  • The move is expected to assist the spread of insurance coverage and benefit the insurance industry.
New Delhi, Oct 4 (PTI) The GST Council is expected to consider a proposal in its meeting on October 7 to allow employers to claim input tax credit on premiums paid for insurance coverage provided to employees.

Under the Goods and Services Tax law, GST is exempt on individuals buying life or health policies for themselves or their family.

However, when a business buys life or health cover for its employees, tax is charged on that cover, and until now the business could not claim ITC on it.


Currently, an 18 per cent GST is levied on all such group insurance policies.

The GST Council will now consider a proposal to allow businesses to claim credit for buying insurance cover for employees. "This will make insurance cheaper for every employer to provide", sources said.

Almost every organised employer provides cover for its workforce, so the cost of providing the coverage comes down, which should assist the spread of cover and the insurance industry itself, sources added.
