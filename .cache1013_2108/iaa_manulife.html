Asia:Manulife Asia appoints new Chief Information Officer

By AIR team | 12 Oct 2026

Asia C-Suite People Life & Health

Manulife Asia has promoted Mr Andy Bruce to the role of Chief Information Officer.

Mr Bruce initially joined Manulife as Chief Information Officer for Hong Kong and Macau, and successfully led one of the market’s largest technology delivery portfolios. He is also widely respected for his strategic leadership, collaborative approach and ability to build high-performing teams while delivering meaningful business outcomes.

According to his LinkedIn profile, Mr Bruce founded Millstar Consultants in 1998. He then worked with BHP Billiton and AIA.
